Import Scout entries, review coverage, and prepare your team’s match thinking from one laptop workspace.

SUPERSCOUT

Laptop workflow
01

Open your workspace

Launch SuperScout and select or create the regional you’re working on. You can keep scouting work on this laptop and use the local workspace when you’re offline.

OverviewRegional workspaceTeam data · Schedule · Coverage
02

Receive Scout data

Open Receive data. Choose Import a scout file for a JSON export, then review the import and select Import into this regional.

For a QR handoff, choose Receive match QR and scan every code shown by Scout. The sender’s QR transfer covers selected match entries only.

Data transferImport a scout fileReceive match QR · Review import
03

Check coverage and teams

Use Schedule & coverage to review the schedule and see which teams have scouting data. Open Overview or Data viewer to inspect imported records and team summaries.

Schedule & coverageTeams and match assignmentsReview what has been scouted
04

Prepare for the next match

Review Match brief and team scouting, then use Superscout and Pick & DNP to organize your team’s strategy. Upload and cloud sync are optional parts of the workflow.
