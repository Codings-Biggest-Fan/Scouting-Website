Scout a match with Scout

Capture what happened, keep your entries on your phone, then pass them to SuperScout when your team is ready.

Phone workflow
01

Open your event

Choose your event and profile. If you’re working without internet, use Start without internet to enter Scout’s offline flow. Your entries stay on this device.

ScoutChoose how to startContinue offline
02

Record the work

From Scout, choose a match to record game observations, or open pit scouting to document a team’s robot. Complete the entry and save it before moving on.

Scout entryMatch observationsRecord actions · Save entry
03

Check your history

Open History to review entries saved for the selected event. Match entries and pit reports remain available for transfer.

HistorySaved entriesMatch scouting · Pit reports
04

Send entries to SuperScout

In Transfer, export one JSON file with all completed match and pit entries from the event. The file can be imported into SuperScout. Your original entries remain saved on the phone.

For QR transfer, select up to three match entries. SuperScout must scan every code shown; codes can arrive in any order. QR transfer does not include pit reports.
